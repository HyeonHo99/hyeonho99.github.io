About Me

I am a researcher at Adobe working on video generation and editing. Currently, I am primarily focused on bringing long video generation and editing capabilities to Video Firefly. I have additionally worked on camera-controllable video generation for Firefly foundational models. Previously at KAIST AI, I worked on improving the controllability and efficiency of video synthesis using diffusion models under the supervision of Prof. Jong Chul Ye.

Adobe Research Internship / University Collaboration: If you are interested in collaborating with me, don't hesitate to reach out via my work email.
Adobe internships are usually discussed around November, December of the year preceding it.
